## What it's meant to do 

Well, organize your computer stuff, of course!

Slots available:
- CYD (Cheap Yellow Displays: 87.5 mm long x 60 mm wide x 17.5 mm thick)
- Raspberry Pi 5 (1)
- Raspberry PI 3 (1)
- Arduino Uno w/ prototype shield coverage (1)
- ESP32 (4)
- ESP32-CAM (5)
- USBs (10)
- MicroSDs (6)
- Extra storage (36x36mm) (Quantity 2)
